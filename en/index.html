HWID — device limits

If your provider limits the number of devices, OutSky will be able to send the device ID such providers require. It’s enabled per subscription.

A random identifier, not a hardware fingerprint. Your provider will also see the OS, its version and the device name (you can change the name). Off by default; the ID can be reset at any time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is OutSky a VPN service?

No. OutSky is a client: it doesn’t sell servers or subscriptions. You need a subscription from your provider or your own server.

Where can I download it?

Not yet — the app is in development. A public alpha for Windows comes first. Follow the news in the @outskyapp channel.

Is it free?

Yes, OutSky is free. You get the server subscription from your provider.

Do I need to sign up?

No. Add a subscription and connect.

My provider limits the number of devices. Will it work?

It should: OutSky will be able to send the device ID (HWID) such providers require. It’s a random identifier, not a hardware fingerprint; along with it your provider will see the OS, its version and the device name. The ID is enabled per subscription and can be reset at any time.

Does the OutSky app collect data?

Not the app itself. It will have no telemetry, analytics or accounts: it won’t collect data about you or how you use it. Subscriptions, settings and logs will stay on your device, and keys, passwords and the device ID will be kept encrypted in the system’s secure storage. Logs will never be sent anywhere on their own: only you will be able to export them, and keys and passwords will be masked in the export. To load your subscription and connect, the app will contact your provider’s servers. As with any VPN client, they see your IP address and the sites you open through the VPN, and may record them along with the app name and version. Your provider will get the device ID, along with the OS, its version and the device name, only if you turn it on for that subscription. If your subscription is from OutSky VPN, that provider is us: the privacy policy describes what we record and what data our bot and the extension process.

Which platforms?

Windows first, then Android and iPhone, later macOS and Linux. For browsers there is already an extension — it works with OutSky VPN.

Will it be open source?

We plan to open-source the code by the first public release.
