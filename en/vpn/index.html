Protocols

In the apps, every country has a main server and backups.

  • VLESS Realitymain — the server named after the country
  • Hysteria2backup — “HS2” in the name
  • xHTTPbackup — “xHTTP” in the name

If the main one won’t connect, try a backup (“HS2” servers don’t work in INCY for Windows yet). In the Chrome extension, just pick a country.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Right now there’s only the free trial: 14 days, 25 GB of traffic, up to 2 devices. Once the 14 days are up or the traffic runs out, the subscription stops working. Paid plans are coming later — we’ll announce them in the @outskyapp channel.

Do I need to sign up?

No separate account needed — Telegram is enough. You get your subscription link from @OutSkyVPN_bot.

How many devices can I use?

Up to two — say, a phone and a computer.

Why subscribe to the channel?

It’s a condition of the free trial. The @outskyapp channel posts news about the service and the OutSky app.

Are OutSky VPN and the OutSky app the same thing?

No. OutSky VPN is a service: servers and a subscription. The OutSky app is a separate free client for any provider’s subscription, and it’s still in development. You can use the service today with INCY or the Chrome extension.

It won’t connect. What now?

Pick a different server or location. Still stuck? Message @outsky_support with your device and what you see on screen — a screenshot helps.

Support & news

Still have questions?

If something isn’t working, message support. News about the service and the OutSky app is posted in the channel.
